World Lung Day is marked globally every year, but public health awareness loses its true purpose when reduced to celebratory banners, empty slogans, or ceremonial walks. For a person living with breathlessness, many such occasions don't mean more than a colourful acknowledgement, that does nothing to alleviate their suffering.

This World Lung Day, the ABK Lung Health Education Portal proposes to take practical steps in improving lung health in the residents of your community and those who live around. Every small step contributed to achieving a healthy lung throughout the life for every person who lives and breathes on mother earth. And one who saves a single life is as though he has saved the whole of humanity.

# 1. Early Life: Safeguarding the Foundations of Breath
A child’s lung reserve establishes the baseline for their entire lifetime. Severe respiratory insults sustained during the first five years of life—whether from poorly managed wheezing, unrecognized infection, or environmental exposure—lead to lifelong structural changes, irreversible airway remodeling, and reduced adult pulmonary function. Preventive programs that target these gaps include childhood immunizations (the Expanded Programme of Immunization (EPI) accessible in all urban and rural government-run medical centers in Pakistan), childhood tuberculosis screening (again available in most health centres) and childhood recognition of chronic lung diseases.

## Practical Steps for Our Community
#### Audit and Support Routine Vaccination: 
*💉 Send 3 children to nearest EPI center for vaccinations.*
Look actively within your extended family, neighborhood, and local domestic staff for under-vaccinated children. Facilitate their transport and registration at the nearest government Expanded Programme on Immunization (EPI) center.

#### Sponsor Acute & Chronic Pediatric Respiratory Care: 
💊 *Directly sponsor the treatment cost—whether oral antibiotics, an MDI inhaler, or a pediatric spacer chamber—for an underprivileged child struggling with acute pneumonia or persistent asthma at your local clinic.*  
Many children suffer from chronic cough, by are unable to get workup. It's not always funds; sometimes it's negligent parents, sometimes it's access, sometimes it's quacks, and sometimes it's myths. 

#### Fund a Specialist Pulmonary Workup for 3 Children: 
*🧑🏼‍⚕️ Identify and sponsor 3 children from deserving families suffering from chronic, unresolved cough to see a qualified Pulmonologist for systematic evaluation*

Discuss with your local practitioner. They may be able to identify children who need workup but are short on finances l. He may suspect asthma, tuberculosis, cystic fibrosis, non-resolving pneumonias, and childhood interstitial lung diseases / Ch-ILDs). Help those children get the  workup.
