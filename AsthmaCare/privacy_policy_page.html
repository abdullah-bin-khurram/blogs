Privacy Policy & Health Data Transparency

⚠️ MANDATORY MEDICAL & EDUCATIONAL DISCLAIMER ABK AsthmaCare is an educational and personal health-tracking tool designed to assist patients in logging peak expiratory flow (PEFR), tracking symptoms, and organizing their asthma action plans. This application is NOT a regulated medical device (Software as a Medical Device - SaMD) and does NOT provide medical diagnosis, clinical decision support, or treatment prescriptions. It is not a substitute for clinical examination or professional medical judgment. Always seek the advice of your pulmonologist, physician, or qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition. In the event of an acute asthma exacerbation or emergency, immediately contact your local emergency response service (e.g., 1122).

1. Core Privacy Principle: 100% On-Device Storage

🔒 Absolute Local Storage Guarantee

Your health records are confidential. ABK AsthmaCare does not operate cloud storage servers, user accounts, or backends. All your clinical logs, peak flow recordings, laboratory test images, and demographic records are stored strictly on your local device inside a secured SQLite database sandbox.

2. Types of Data Processed Locally

The App allows you to record and review the following personal and health-related data:

  • Demographic Information: Name, age, gender, height, weight, calculated BMI, and preferred language.
  • Respiratory & PEFR Logs: Peak Expiratory Flow Rate (L/min) readings, percentage of personal best, symptom zone categorizations (Green, Yellow, Red), and environmental triggers.
  • Spirometry & Diagnostic Results: Manually recorded spirometry parameters (FEV1, FVC, FEV1/FVC ratio) and symptom scores based on Global Initiative for Asthma (GINA) guidelines.
  • Attached Medical Documents: Photos of lab tests, chest X-rays, or prescriptions that you manually capture or select from your gallery.
  • Medication & Inhaler Schedules: Names of prescribed inhalers, dosage intervals, medication adherence check-offs, and doctor appointment dates.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

ABK AsthmaCare only requests Android permissions strictly necessary for its features:

  • Approximate Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used solely to fetch city-level Air Quality Index (AQI) and PM2.5 levels from Open-Meteo. Exact GPS coordinates are not collected, logged, or shared.
  • Notifications & Exact Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to fire local reminders for medication doses and upcoming clinic appointments.
  • Camera & Photo Access (CAMERA, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used only when you choose to take or attach a picture of your lab result or radiology film.
  • Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to fetch external environmental air quality data, display standard ads, and process Google Play in-app subscriptions.

4. Advertising & Health Data Protection

The free tier of ABK AsthmaCare integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banners and occasional interstitial ads. In strict adherence to Google Play policies:

  • Zero Health Data Sharing: Your medical measurements, symptoms, PEFR values, and personal profiles are never shared with Google AdMob or any ad network.
  • No Behavioral Profiling: The App does not build behavioral health profiles or serve targeted ads based on your medical condition.
  • Ad-Free Option: You can completely eliminate advertisements and all ad-network network requests by subscribing to the Ad-Free version via Google Play In-App Purchase.

5. Data Sharing & User Initiated Exports

We do not sell, rent, or share personal or health data. Data leaves your device only when you explicitly select an export feature, such as generating a PDF Doctor Visit Summary or Spirometry Report to share with your personal healthcare team via WhatsApp, email, or other apps.

6. Data Retention, Control, and Deletion

You have full autonomy over your records:

  • Selective Deletion: You can delete personal profile records via Settings > Data Privacy & Storage > Delete Personal Data.
  • Complete Reset: You can choose Reset All to purge all databases, logs, and diagnostic images immediately.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the application completely erases all stored data from your device's storage sandbox.
