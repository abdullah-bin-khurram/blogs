⚠️ IMPORTANT MEDICAL & CLINICAL DISCLAIMER

ABK AsthmaCare is an educational and personal self-monitoring digital log designed to assist individuals and parents in tracking asthma symptoms, logging peak expiratory flow (PEFR), and reviewing personalized action plans.

NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE: This Application is NOT a certified Software as a Medical Device (SaMD), diagnostic tool, or clinical decision support software. It does NOT diagnose illnesses, predict clinical emergencies, or formulate medical treatment regimens.

NO DOCTOR-PATIENT RELATIONSHIP: Use of this Application does not establish a physician-patient relationship. Calculations such as GINA symptom control scores and predicted PEFR percentages are provided strictly for informational and conversational reference with your licensed physician or pulmonologist.

EMERGENCY PROTOCOL: Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking clinical examination because of information presented in this Application. If you or a loved one experiences severe shortness of breath, inability to speak full sentences, blue discoloration of the lips or fingernails, or peak flow drops into the Red Zone (<50%), immediately contact emergency medical services (such as 1122 in Pakistan, 911, or your local emergency hospital).

2. Scope & Core Architecture

🔒 100% On-Device Local Sandbox Architecture

Unlike standard cloud-based health portals, ABK AsthmaCare is designed with a privacy-by-default, offline-first philosophy. We do not operate cloud databases, user registration servers, or remote repositories for your medical files. All demographic profiles, respiratory logs, spirometry records, lab report images, and inhaler schedules remain stored strictly on your physical device within the app's sandboxed SQLite database (abk_lung_health.db).

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application ABK AsthmaCare (referred to as "the Application", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), authored and published by Dr. Abdullah bin Khurram. It governs the handling of data collected when you install, access, or use the Application on Android devices.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on which features of the Application you choose to utilize, the following categories of information are processed on your device:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Personal Demographics: Name, age, gender, height (cm/in), weight (kg/lbs), calculated Body Mass Index (BMI), emergency contact phone number, treating doctor’s name, clinic contact, and preferred language (English or Urdu).
  • Respiratory & Peak Flow (PEFR) Logs: Peak Expiratory Flow Rate readings (measured in Liters per minute - L/min), baseline reference values (Personal Best or calculated predicted value using European/Godfrey formulas), percentage of personal best, symptom zones (Green Zone: 80–100%, Yellow Zone: 50–80%, Red Zone: <50%), and associated timestamps.
  • Diagnostic Spirometry Metrics: Manually recorded spirometry parameters including Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 second (FEV1), Forced Vital Capacity (FVC), FEV1/FVC ratio, and post-bronchodilator reversibility test measurements.
  • Asthma Symptoms & GINA Control Scores: Responses to standard Global Initiative for Asthma (GINA) symptom assessment criteria (daytime symptoms frequency, night-time awakenings, rescue inhaler usage, and activity limitations).
  • Exacerbation & Flare-Up History: Records of acute asthma exacerbation events, documented environmental triggers (e.g., dust, pollen, cold air, smoke, viral infections, exercise), oral corticosteroid courses, and hospital or emergency room visit notes.
  • Medication & Inhaler Tracking: Inhaler brand and generic names, inhaler category (controller/preventer vs. reliever/rescue), prescribed dose and frequency, medication intake adherence logs, and scheduled follow-up clinic appointment dates.
  • Medical Images & Documentation: Photos or digital copies of physical laboratory test results (e.g., complete blood counts, eosinophil levels, total serum IgE, allergy panels) and radiology films (chest X-rays, HRCT scans) that you voluntarily capture or upload.

B. Information Collected Automatically

When using the Application, minimal technical data is processed automatically to maintain operational functionality:

  • Device & OS Specifications: Device model, manufacturer, Android OS version, system language, and display resolution.
  • App Performance & Preferences: User settings stored locally (e.g., dark/light mode preference, measurement units, notification toggles, and interstitial ad cooldown timers).

5. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal and health information solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Personal Health Tracking: To generate visual PEFR trends, symptom zone alerts, medication adherence histories, and spirometry charts on your screen.
  • Educational Assessment: To summarize your self-reported symptoms against published clinical guidelines (GINA 4-week assessment) and standard PEFR reference equations.
  • Reminder Delivery: To fire timely local alerts reminding you to take prescribed controller medications or attend clinic follow-up visits.
  • Environmental Context: To display live local Air Quality Index (AQI) values to help you anticipate potential respiratory triggers.
  • Report Generation: To format your logged readings into PDF summaries (such as the Doctor Visit Summary or Spirometry Report) when you choose to export them.
  • Service Maintenance & Security: To resolve technical issues and preserve database integrity across app upgrades.
🚫 NO AD PROFILING OR HEALTH DATA COMMERCIALIZATION Under no circumstances is your personal health data (PEFR values, symptoms, medications, or diagnostic images) sold, rented, monetized, or shared with advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing entities.

8. Data Sharing & User Initiated Exports

We do not sell, transfer, or disclose your personal or medical records to third parties. Information leaves your device exclusively through user-initiated actions:

  • PDF Export & Sharing: When you generate a Doctor Visit Summary or Spirometry Report, the PDF file is created locally on your device. When you tap "Share", the file is handed to your operating system's standard share sheet (e.g., WhatsApp, Gmail, Bluetooth, or AirDrop) based strictly on your selection.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information only if required by law, subpoena, or valid court order from competent law enforcement authorities, to the extent such records exist. (Note: Because data is stored locally, we do not possess remote copies of your records).

9. Storage & Data Security

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal and health records:

  • Operating System Sandboxing: All data is stored within Android’s secure app sandbox. Other applications installed on your phone cannot access ABK AsthmaCare’s SQLite database or stored document images without rooted access.
  • No Cloud Intermediaries: By avoiding remote server storage, your records are immune to third-party database breaches, cloud server leaks, or mass unauthorized access.
  • Encrypted Transport: When external requests occur (fetching live AQI data from Open-Meteo or verifying subscription tokens with Google Play), all communications are secured via HTTPS (Transport Layer Security - TLS 1.3).

10. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because data is stored on-device, you maintain continuous and total authority over its retention and deletion:

  • Demographic Deletion: In the App, navigate to Settings > Data Privacy & Storage > Delete Personal Data. This immediately erases your name, age, gender, contact numbers, doctor info, and address while keeping your medical logs intact.
  • Complete Factory Reset: In Settings > Data Privacy & Storage > Factory Reset, you can execute a full wipe. This action completely deletes all database tables, PEFR logs, spirometry records, lab report images, medication histories, appointments, and notification schedules.
  • Application Removal: Uninstalling the Application from your Android device causes the operating system to permanently purge all sandboxed database files and associated documents.

12. Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for use by adults or under direct adult supervision. We do not knowingly collect, transmit, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions) to remote servers. All pediatric metrics entered by parents or legal guardians remain strictly on the parent’s device. If you believe a child has provided personal information without parental consent, you can immediately erase it via the App's Factory Reset button or contact us at hafizhoot@gmail.com.
