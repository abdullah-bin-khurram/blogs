  Track your asthma with your peak flow meter.


  This simple daily chart helps you keep an eye on how well your lungs are working. By recording your peak flow readings regularly, you and your doctor can understand your usual breathing pattern, see whether your asthma is well controlled, and notice early changes that may warn of worsening asthma—even before you start feeling unwell.
